ABOUT AFM (ADVANCED FINANCIAL MODELER)

AFM Overview The AFM accreditation is the flagship program of the Financial Modeling Institute (FMI). This prestigious certification is renowned globally for its rigorous standards and comprehensive curriculum. AFM accreditation holders have not only learned but also demonstrated their ability to construct top-tier 3-statement financial models. These models are meticulously designed to avoid common errors, highlighting the holder’s remarkable skills and disciplined approach to financial modeling. AFM “X” Factor: Winning Proposition Upon passing the AFM exam, successful candidates receive a digital badge to highlight and validate their financial modeling skills. Those who achieve the AFM designation can use the letters “AFM” in their signature and social media pages online Those who pass the AFM assessment will be included in FMI’s Directory of Accreditation holders *source Once you attain the AFM accreditation, you will be able to... Validate your ability to build a three-statement model of a company from scratch. Create a model that can be used as a critical decision-making tool, allowing users to make accurate and informed financial decisions.
