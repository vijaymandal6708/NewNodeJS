PROGRAM OVERVIEW

Global Financial Modeling Program is a comprehensive certification program designed for professionals to pursue distinction in Financial Modeling that validates their skills at a global level. With a growing economy and increasing demand for financial analysts, there are expanding career prospects for talented professionals in sectors like investment banking, consulting, and corporate finance, among others. NSE Academy in partnership with Financial Modeling Institute (FMI) is offering the Global Financial Modeling Program. This program empowers finance and accounting professionals to develop and validate important modeling skills needed for creating accurate financial forecasts and supporting critical business decisions.
